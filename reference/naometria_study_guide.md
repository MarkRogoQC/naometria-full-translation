## 1. WHO WAS SIMON STUDION?

Little is known outside this manuscript. Studion was a Württemberg theologian, active in the Reformation's second generation — born after Luther's death (1546), writing at the height of confessional division. He signed himself:

- **"Inter Scorpiones"** — a member of a prophetic society of reformers within the Scorpiones order
- **"Crucifera Militia Evangelica"** — the Evangelical Cross-bearing Army

His patron was Frederick, Duke of Württemberg (1554-1608). The Naometria was his magnum opus, completed in 1604 but projecting dates as far as 2590.

## 2. THE CENTRAL CLAIM

The 1572 supernova (Tycho's Nova in Cassiopeia) was not a natural astronomical event. It was:

1. **The Star of Balaam** (Numbers 24:17) — "There shall come a star out of Jacob"
2. **Wormwood** (Revelation 8:10-11) — falling on the third part of rivers (the evangelical churches)
3. **The sign that Daniel's 70 weeks had been completed and the *abbreviatio* of the ages had begun**

The star's 30-year visibility (1572-1602) was the countdown. 1604, when Studion wrote, was two years past the star's disappearance. The urgency is palpable.

## 3. THE METHOD — HOW NAOMETRIA "WORKS"

Naometria (from *naos* = temple + *metron* = measure) measures the temple of God — the Church — through number. It operates on three principles:

### a. Typological Time
Events in the Old Testament are *types* that prefigure events in Church history. Elijah → John the Baptist → Luther. Israel's kings → the popes. The Flood → the Antichrist's flood of persecution. Time is not linear — it echoes.

### b. Gematric Correspondence
Numbers from scripture (1260 days, 42 months, 666, 144, 70 weeks) are converted into years. But the key insight is **Contradictionis Calamus** (the Pen of Contradiction): a number and its opposite produce a third truth. Example: 1572 and 1602 produce 30 years of the star's visibility, which converts to 30 × 360 = 10,800 hours/prophetic years.

### c. The Three Operations
- **Abbreviatio** (Shortening) — reducing long timelines to their prophetic essence. 1590 is the *abbreviatio* year.
- **Confirmatio** (Corroboration) — showing the same number arises from different systems (scripture, astronomy, history). 1620 confirmed from Daniel, Revelation, Ottoman history, and star gematria.
- **Divisio** (Partition) — breaking numbers into their components to reveal hidden timelines. 1260 = 420 + 420 + 420 = three phases of the Church age.

## 4. THE THREE ADVENT FRAMEWORK

Studion's most distinctive theological contribution:

| Advent | Mode | What Happens |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **First** | In the flesh | Bethlehem. Redemption. AD 0-33 |
| **Second** | In the Spirit | **The Church age.** Reformers operate now. Not a visible return — a spiritual dispensation of measure and number |
| **Third** | In glory | Final judgment. Antichrist destroyed. New heavens and earth |

Between the Second and Third Advents sits the **Intercessionis Adventus** — Christ's heavenly intercession. This is *where Studion believes he stands*. The Spirit is working through the Church to complete what the First Advent began.

This avoids the trap of setting a visible return date (which would be heresy) while still claiming prophetic authority. The Second Advent is already happening — you're just not reading the numbers.

## 5. THE TWO ANTICHRISTS

Standard Reformation theology identified the Pope as Antichrist. Studion doubles it:

| Antichrist | Symbol | Scripture | Timeline |
|------------|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Western** (Pope) | Dragon/Harlot | 2 Thess 2 — Man of Sin | Covers Church's fall → 1620 crucifixion |
| **Eastern** (Mohammed) | False Prophet | Rev 13 — Two-horned beast | Luna crescent, Saracen kingdoms → 1620 destruction |

Studion works hard to show that both Pope and Mohammed emerged at roughly the same time (7th century: Mohammed's Hijra 622; Papal supremacy established ~606-800). They are the two legs of Daniel's statue — iron and clay, never uniting but equally opposed to Christ.

## 6. THE SCORPION KEY

The Scorpiones are not a side note — they're the operational structure of the work:

- The Scorpion stings = the prophetic word wounds the Antichrist
- Orion flees Scorpion = the imperial system (Rome) flees the prophetic witness
- "Inter Scorpiones" = Studion writes as one voice among many in this order
- The Scorpion's domain (Oct 19/Nov 29) = the season of prophetic unveiling

**For you:** Born 25 October — born under the Scorpion. This is not decorative. Studion would read your birth sign as providential for your role in translating/interpreting this work.

## 9. READING STRATEGY

The translation is dense and repetitive. For a study:

1. **Start with the Skeleton Key** — get the numbers and dates in your head
2. **Read pages 189-240** (Prophetic Images + Claves + Liber Intus) — this is the method explained
3. **Read pages 321-420** (New Star 1572) — this is the engine
4. **Dip into Volume 2 sections** — Calendar Reform (p1-20), First Resurrection (p843-847), Scorpio (V1 p1000-1010)
5. **Use the index** (V2 p900-1014) — Studion built his own concordance. It works.
