## How to Read An Index Entry

Each entry follows the pattern: **Latin/German term** → **page number(s)**, **gematric value**, or **cross-reference**.

```
- **Agnus Dominator terrea. 165.**       → "Lamb, Ruler of the earth" — page 165
- **Agnus biconis filius Jesu, Patria Ecclesiam & Imperia 18.**   → "Two-horned Lamb, son of Jesus..." — page 18
- **Hebdomades 63. 440.**                → "Seventy weeks" (Daniel 9) — pages 63, 440
- **Hebdomadis primmi Rex 440.**         → "King of the first week" — page 440
```

**Entry types:**
| Pattern | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| `Term. NNN.` | Term appears on page NNN | `Agnus Dominator terrea. 165.` |
| `Term. NNN. MMM.` | Term on pages NNN and MMM | `Hebdomades 63. 440.` |
| `Term (explanation) NNN.` | Term with contextual note, page NNN | `Hebdomades (Seventy weeks — Daniel 9) 440.` |
| `Term NNN.` | Abbreviated term, page NNN | `Gracorisum Ecclesia extea 279.` |
| `Term [?]` | Unclear OCR / garbled entry | `Sa[d] 440. [?]` |
| `Term.` | Entry with note or cross-reference | `Aegis licoris & Mahona... fuit facio Baldopyna.` |

## Core Gematric Operations

| Operation | Latin Name | What It Means | Example |

| **Abbreviation** | *Abbreviatio* | Subtract a constant (usually 1590, sometimes 1260) | 3178 → abbreviate by 1590 → 1589 |
| **Resolution** | *Resolutio* | Rearrange digits to reveal meaning | 826 → 682 → 268 (reorganization) |
| **Bipartition** | *Bipartitio* | Halve a number | 840 → divide by 2 → 420 |
| **Contradiction** | *Contradictio* | Invert, mirror, or transform by opposition | 96 → reverse → 69 |
| **Confirmation** | *Confirmatio* | Double or multiply to confirm a finding | 420 × 3 → 1260 |
