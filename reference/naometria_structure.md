# Naometria — Structural Outline

## Overview

**Full Title:** *Naometria, seu Nuda et Prima Libri Intus et Foris*
**Author:** Simon Studion (1543–1605)
**Date:** 1604 (begun ca. 1590s)
**Interlocutors:** Nathanael & Clephas
**Language:** Latin (with Greek and Hebrew insertions)
**Two Volumes:** Pars Prior (Vol. 1, pp. 9–1115) & Pars Posterior (Vol. 2, pp. 1–1025)

---

## VOLUME 1 — PARS PRIOR (Pages 9–1115)

### I. FRONT MATTER & DEDICATION (pp. 9–18)

#### 1. Title Page (p. 9)
**Content type:** Title / Prologue
**Key text:** "NAOMETRIA SEV Nüida, et prima Libri intus, et forcis … PARS PRIOR — Intercolléditores Nathanaël Clephas, ANNO 1604. Consistorium."

#### 2. Dedication to Frederick, Duke of Württemberg (pp. 11–14)
**Content type:** Dedicatory epistle
**Key text:** "ILLVSTRISSIMO PRINCIPI, ac Domino, Domino Friderico, Duci Wirttenbergico… Domino suo Clementissimo. S.P.D."

#### 3. Opening Exposition: Elijah Typology & Lutheran Reformation (pp. 11–60)
**Content type:** Theological-gematric exposition
Elijah as type of John the Baptist and Martin Luther; Augsburg Confession (1530) as spiritual Jordan crossing; 1260 days of Revelation 11–12; Pharaoh as Antichrist type; Ahab/Jezebel as papal types; gematric calculations linking 1517, 1521, 1530.

---

### II. GEMATRIC EXPOSITION (pp. 61–188)

#### 4. Saul, David, and Kingdom Chronology (pp. 60–72)
**Content type:** Gematric / typological
Saul's rejection = papal rejection; David's anointing = Protestant rise; "Virga ferrea" (iron rod); AM 2883 to AM 3963 (Christ's birth).

#### 5. The Ottoman Empire and Eschatological Calculations (pp. 73–82)
**Content type:** Gematric / political prophecy
Mohammed III as eastern Antichrist; Constantinople 1453; Rodolph II and Philip II; years 1575, 1576, 1582, 1590.

#### 6. The Comet of 1577 and Calendar Reform (pp. 83–90)
**Content type:** Celestial/chronological
New Star of 1572; Gregorian Calendar 1582; Heinrich IV as "coming from Edom" (Isaiah 63).

#### 7. The Valley of Jehoshaphat and Gideon Typology (pp. 91–120)
**Content type:** Gematric / typological
Valley of Jehoshaphat (Joel 3); Gideon's 300; Madianites = Saracens; Heinrich IV's 1553 birth; Babylon's fall ~1620-1621.

#### 8. Jacob's Ladder (pp. 121–140)
**Content type:** Gematric / mystical
Scala hebraice Selam; 130 years; Stuttgart as sacred geography; Reformation chronology.

#### 9. The Dragon, France, and Spain (pp. 140–180)
**Content type:** Typological / political
Dragon = Roman Emperor; France/England Protestant alliance; year 1612 as papal crucifixion.

#### 10. The Evangelical Militia and Closing Prayer (pp. 175–187)
**Content type:** Prayer / peroration
"Sit Dominus tecum… Datum, diad. Idus Novembr. Anno 1604. … Simon Studion."

---

### III. PROPHETIC IMAGES (pp. 189–200)

#### 11. Three Ancient Prophetic Images (pp. 189–200)
**Content type:** Visual prophecy with exegesis
"Vaticinæ, et antiqvè imagines tres"
- Image 1 (p.189): Crowned figure with scales, woman on horseback — Christ's judgment, Church militant
- Image 2 (p.190): "Imago Joachinica" — geometric/compass Joachimite figure
- Image 3 (pp.191–200): Four Beasts with Roman imperial interpretation; S.P.Q.R. symbolism

---

### IV. COROLLARIUM NAOMETRIAE (pp. 201–216)

#### 12. Corollarium: Frederick II, Henry of Navarre, Frederick of Württemberg (pp. 201–216)
**Content type:** Typological comparison / genealogical-gematric
"Corollarium Naometriae. De Friderico secundo… cum Heinrich 4. Navarrae & Franciae regis; et … Friderici Ducis Wirtembergici"
Three figures bound by gematric numbers 1196, 1553, 1554.

#### 13. The New Star of 1572 and Sardanapalus Typology (pp. 203–216)
**Content type:** Celestial / historical typology
New Star 1572; Sardanapalus = papal Rome's fall; pivotal years 1606, 1613, 1621.

---

### V. PARS PRIOR TITLE RESTATEMENT (pp. 217–218)

#### 14. Title Restatement (p. 217)
**Content type:** Title page (internal)
"Naometria Novæ et prognostici pars prior. Authore Simone Studione, inter Scorpiones. Anno 1604. Intercollactores, Nathanael, Cleophas."

---

### VI. CLAVIS SCIENTIAE & CLAVIS DAVIDIS (pp. 219–229)

#### 15. Clavis Scientiae — Key of Science (pp. 219–220)
**Content type:** Hermeneutical dialogue
Nathanael and Cleophas dialogue on unlocking Scripture's prophecies through numerical demonstration.

#### 16. Clavis Davidis — Key of David (pp. 220–222)
**Content type:** Hermeneutical method
"Quid est Clavis David?" — Universal key for investigating Scripture and nature from Creation to eschaton. Operates through gematric *calamus* (resolution, contradiction, confirmation).

---

### VII. LIBER INTUS ET FORIS SCRIPTUS (pp. 222–235)

#### 17. The Seven-Sealed Book: Formation (pp. 222–225)
**Content type:** Naometric methodology
"Liber intus & foris scriptus." Foundational number: **1260** (Revelation 12:6). Through division into 420+420+420 and recombination, the Book's numeric structure is generated. Seven seals = seven fundamental naometric numbers.

#### 18. Quatuor Animalia — The Four Beasts (pp. 226–231)
**Content type:** Symbolic-astrological-typological system
"Quid sibi volunt quatuor animalia" — Four seasons of cosmic year; four tribes (Reuben/South/Man, Judah/East/Ox, Dan/North/Eagle, Ephraim/West/Lion); four Church ages.

#### 19. Liber Apertionis: Year of Opening (pp. 231–235)
**Content type:** Chronological calculation
When the Book opens; *Abbreviatio* concept; year 1590 as year of abbreviation/opening.

---

### VIII. CORPUS NAOMETRICUM — CORE OPERATIONS (pp. 233–240+)

#### 20. Abbreviatio, Confirmatio, Divisio (pp. 233–240+)
**Content type:** Methodological primer
Three core naometric operations: Abbreviatio (shortening of days, Matthew 24:22), Confirmatio (prophetic corroboration, Daniel 9), Divisio (before/after Christ partitioning).

---

### IX. CONTINUED GEMATRIC EXPOSITION (pp. 241–1115)

#### 21. Chronological-Gematric Calculations (pp. 241–700)
**Content type:** Dense gematric/chronological
Continued numerical resolutions; papal fall calculations; Houses of Württemberg, Navarre, Habsburg; astrological data; eschatological projections to 1606, 1612–1613, 1620–1621.

#### 22. Tabular Sections & Summaries (pp. 701–1115)
**Content type:** Tabular gematric / summaries
Systematic tables; generational chronologies Adam-to-present; AM/AUC/AD concordances; key year-symbol summaries.

## Summary Statistics

- **Total pages:** ~2,132 (Vol 1: ~1,107 + Vol 2: ~1,025)
- **Major sections:** 26
- **Core operations:** Abbreviatio, Confirmatio, Divisio (plus Clavis Davidis and Clavis Scientiae)
- **Key numbers:** 1260 (foundational), 420/420/420 (division), 1604, 1606, 1608 (projections)
- **Format:** Dedication → Gematric exposition → Prophetic images → Clavis keys → Chronological tables → Alphabetical index
- **Alphabetical index:** ~2,923 name→number entries, pages 943–1014 (~70 pages), the densest frequency concentration in the work
