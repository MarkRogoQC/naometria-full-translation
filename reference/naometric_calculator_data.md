## KEY OPERATIONS

### Abbreviatio (Shortening)
- Date - Root = Abbreviation
- Example: 1620 - 30 = 1590
- Example: 2590 - 1000 = 1590

### Confirmatio (Corroboration)
Same number from different systems:
- 1620 from Revelation (1260+360)
- 1620 from Daniel (1290+330)
- 1620 from Star (1572+48)
- 1620 from Scorpion (3240/2)

### Divisio (Partition)
Breaking numbers:
- 1260 = 420 + 420 + 420
- 3240 = 1080 + 600 + 1560
- 360 = 6 × 60 = 12 × 30

### Hour↔Year Conversion
- 1 hour = 15 degrees
- 1 day = 24 hours
- 1 prophetic day = 1 year
- 360 days = 360 years
- 1 hour = 1 year in abbreviated form
