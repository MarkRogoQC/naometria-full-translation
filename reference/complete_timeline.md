## Key to Chronological Systems Used

Studion employs multiple interlocking chronological systems throughout the _Naometria_:

| System | Abbreviation | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Anno Mundi** | AM | Year of the World, counting from Creation |
| **Anno Christi / Domini** | AD / AC | Year of Christ / Year of the Lord (calculated from Christ's birth) |
| **Ante natum Christum** | BC | Years before Christ's birth (varies from modern BC) |
| **Anno abbreviationis** | AA | Year of Abbreviation (a prophetic count beginning at a key point) |
| **Anno Romae conditae** | AR | Year from the founding of Rome |
| **Anno peregrinationis Abrahae** | APA | Year from Abraham's pilgrimage/wandering |

### Conversion Anchors (Studion's system):

- AM 1 = Creation = ~3963 BC (Studion's variant)
- AM 2454 = Exodus = 1509 BC
- AM 2950 = Abraham born
- AM 3962–3963 = Christ's birth / Christ begins ministry
- AM 5595 = From restoration through Christ to 2590 AD
- AD 1572 = Nova Stella (Tycho Brahe's supernova in Cassiopeia)
- AD 1590 = **Annus Abbreviationis** (terminal abbreviation year)
- AD 1602 = **Cognata Stella** (companion star)
- AD 1604 = Naometria composed
- AD 1620 = Predicted fall of Babylon (Papacy) / Nova Reformatio
- AD 1878 = Prophetic future date
- AD 2590 = **Annus Christi** — New Creation / new age of righteousness
- AD 5557 = Mystical endpoint
